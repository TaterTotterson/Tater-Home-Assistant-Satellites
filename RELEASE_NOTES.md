# Tater Satellite 1.4.37

## What's changed

- **Manage saved Studio designs:** Move your designs higher on the start page, with Continue and Delete on every card and a saved-design count. Add All designs and Delete design at the top of an open workspace, plus a Manage designs shortcut in errors. Confirm deletion and keep published Home Assistant dashboards intact.
- **Keep completed section repairs:** Accept usable sections when a model returns a full dashboard instead of the focused repair format, or encodes layout containers as JSON text. Merge only the requested sections and preserve other views and view options.
- **Repair the remaining content:** Keep successful repairs when another view is still incomplete. Give the next attempt its previous response and precise field paths, and clarify the repair tool's instructions and examples. Continue validating the result and leave the saved draft unchanged if repair fails.

All **617 automated tests** pass, including saved-design deletion, freeing a full Studio slot, full-dashboard repair responses, partial repair progress, and draft protection.

Update through Marketplace or HACS, restart Home Assistant, then refresh the bridge page. **No satellite firmware update is required.**
